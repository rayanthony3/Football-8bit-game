# Privacy Policy — GridIron 8-Bit

**Last Updated:** June 2025

## Overview
GridIron 8-Bit is an offline mobile game. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection
GridIron 8-Bit does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The game:
- Runs entirely on your device
- Does not require an internet connection
- Does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
- Does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or location
- Does not create user accounts
- Does not share any data with third parties

## Game Data
All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device and never leave it.

## Children
This game is appropriate for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Contact
For questions about this privacy policy, contact: privacy@gridiron8bit.example.com

## Changes
If we update this policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date above.
